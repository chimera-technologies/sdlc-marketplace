# Release: v1.1.0

## Summary
Customers can see the status of their open claims in the portal, with the
next step for each, instead of calling the claims line.

## Changes

|---|---|---|

## Risk and rollback
Risk medium (spec `claims-status-self-service`): a new read-only endpoint
and page behind the `claim-status` feature flag; no data migration.
Rollback: switch the flag off, or redeploy v1.0.0 with a rollback release.
